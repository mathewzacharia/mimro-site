About MIMRO

ܡܳܪܝ ܐܰܦܪܶܝܡ ܡܰܕܪܶܫܬܳܐ ܕܪܶܟܢܳܐ ܐܰܠܳܗܳܝܳܐ

MIMRO is a Centre for Languages, Liturgy and Life Sciences, founded in 2008. After eight years of successful service, it was registered in 2016 as a charitable trust named the MIMRO Foundation Trust.

Our motto

We aim to bring in and share the similarities and basic oneness of the world's languages, and to bring unity through the link of liturgy.

Vision

The purpose of the MIMRO Foundation Trust is to fulfil the Gospel of Salvation of Jesus Christ; to aid deserving helpless people and educated unemployed people in society and channel the support they need; and to provide treatment facilities at minimum or no cost.

Mission

  • Preserving and spreading the Syriac language and liturgy, including liturgical studies in Arabic, Hebrew and Greek.
  • Opportunities to study Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Telugu and Sanskrit.
  • Seminars to introduce European languages such as German, French, Italian, Spanish and English.
  • Teaching aids and e-books that make complicated mathematical ideas easier to understand.
  • Free food at government hospitals.
  • Seminars and programmes that draw on Gandhian principles.
  • Opportunities for organic farming.
  • Housing projects: building homes for the poor and deprived.
  • Electronic and computer parks.
  • Tourism projects, and workshops for making, exhibiting and marketing handicrafts.
  • Training classes for disaster management.
  • Awards, fellowships and scholarships for achievers in different fields.
