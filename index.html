About MIMRO

MIMRO is a centre for languages, liturgy and life sciences, founded in 2008. After eight years of service it was registered in 2016 as a charitable trust, the MIMRO Foundation Trust.

Our motto is to share the oneness of the world's languages and to bring unity through the link of liturgy. Read our vision and mission.

Begin with the 22 letters
